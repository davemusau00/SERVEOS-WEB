# Receive a delivery

Section: Procurement
Roles: Admin, Manager, Storekeeper
Permission: procurement.view, procurement.receive
Screen: procurement
Guide: stock.receive
Keywords: receive, delivery, purchase order, supplier

## Overview

Record the quantities that arrived against the supplier or approved purchase order.

## Procedure

1. Open Procurement and choose the order or supplier.
2. Enter the delivery reference and receiving location.
3. Review each delivered and rejected quantity and the item cost.
4. Confirm the receipt only after checking it against the physical delivery.

## What ServOS handles

The API records the goods receipt and related stock and payable changes in a transaction.

## Common mistakes and correction

Do not mark ordered quantities as received when they did not arrive. Record a correction through the relevant reviewed workflow.
