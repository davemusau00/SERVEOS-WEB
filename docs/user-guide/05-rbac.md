# Staff access

Section: Staff
Roles: Admin, Manager
Permission: staff.view, staff.create, staff.update, staff.deactivate
Screen: settings
Keywords: staff, role, permissions, account

## Overview

Staff accounts and their permission grants are managed through the API.

## Procedure

1. Open Staff administration in Settings.
2. Review the staff member and the permissions your account may manage.
3. Enter a reason when creating, changing or deactivating an account.
4. Give a new staff member their login securely and require them to set a password when prompted.

## What ServOS handles

The API checks the administrator's grant ceiling and records staff and device changes.

## Common mistakes and correction

Do not share passwords or include credentials in a CSV import. Ask an administrator to restore an account if the wrong staff member was changed.
