# Discounts and Comps

Section: Discounts & Comps
Roles: Admin, Manager
Permission: order.discount
Screen: pos
Keywords: discounts and comps, discounts & comps, ServOS

## Overview

Discounts and comps are protected, reasoned and audited.

## Procedure

For a discount enter the percentage and reason. A comp targets one order item and keeps its stock consumption intact. Operators can request a manager PIN approval; the approval is single-use and attached to the audit.

## What ServOS records

Actions that change the business are committed through the native backend. When applicable, business records, audit evidence and synchronization outbox entries commit together.

## Common mistakes and correction

Do not treat a button, toast or browser preview as proof that a business transaction was committed. Correct mistakes through the documented reversal, void, refund, count or manager-approved workflow rather than deleting historical transactions.
