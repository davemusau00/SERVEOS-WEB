# Customer credit

Section: Finance
Roles: Admin, Manager, Accountant
Permission: credit.view, credit.charge, credit.settle
Screen: finance
Keywords: customer credit, charge, settlement, balance

## Overview

Customer credit is a receivable ledger, separate from cash tender.

## Procedure

1. Select the customer account and review its status and limit.
2. Record a credit charge only when the sale is approved for credit.
3. For a settlement, select the tender received and review its allocation.
4. Review discrepancies in the reconciliation workflow.

## What ServOS handles

The API records each ledger entry, settlement allocation and reconciliation event.

## Common mistakes and correction

Do not count a credit charge as cash received or change an old balance directly. Resolve it through the ledger workflow.
