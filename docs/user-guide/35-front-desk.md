# Front desk

Section: Property
Roles: Admin, Manager, Front Desk
Permission: rooms.view, rooms.operate, folio.view
Screen: frontdesk
Keywords: arrivals, check-in, stay, checkout, folio

## Overview

Front Desk supports the guest's arrival, stay and checkout workflows.

## Procedure

1. Find the reservation and verify the guest and dates.
2. Review room readiness and any maintenance block.
3. Confirm check-in or room move only after the guest and room are verified.
4. Review folio entries and outstanding balance before checkout.

## What ServOS handles

The API validates the reservation and records the stay and folio transitions.

## Common mistakes and correction

Do not check in an unverified guest or release an unfinished maintenance block.
