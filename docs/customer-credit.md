# Customer credit

Customer credit is a dedicated API ledger. A credit sale records the receivable separately from cash or other tender. Settlements allocate against the original credit entries and preserve the resulting audit and accounting records.

Credit limits, account status, charges, settlements, write offs and reconciliation actions require their respective permissions and server checks. A statement variance remains a discrepancy until an authorized resolution command records its outcome.

Never treat an open credit balance as cash received. Use the [POS workflow](pos.md) for sales and the [finance guide](finance-assets.md) for accounting reports.
